The Secret Agent's Guide to
Direct & Indirect Speech

Learn how to report what people say like a pro, without changing the true meaning!

What's the Difference?

Imagine you are a secret agent or a news reporter. Your job is to tell people what someone else said. You have two ways to do this!

Method 1

Direct Speech 🎤

You repeat the exact words the person said. You trap their words inside quotation marks (" ").

Tom said, "I am eating a giant pizza!"

Method 2

Indirect (Reported) Speech 🕵️‍♂️

You tell the meaning of what they said, but without the exact words and without quotation marks.

Tom said that he was eating a giant pizza.

"I love grammar!"

The 3 Golden Rules of Transformation 🏆

1

Drop the Punctuation

Remove the commas (,) and quotation marks (" "). Often, we use the magical word "that" to glue the sentence together.

," " ➡️ that
2

Change the Pronouns

Because you are reporting it, the perspective changes. "I" becomes "he/she", "we" becomes "they".

I ➡️ He / She
My ➡️ His / Her
3

Step Back in Time

If the person said it in the past, the action belongs in the past! Tenses usually step one level backward.

The Time Machine (Tense Changes)

Memorize this trick: Present becomes Past. Past becomes Past Perfect!

Direct Speech Tense Indirect Speech Tense Example (He said, ...)
Simple Present
(write/writes)		 ➡️ Simple Past
(wrote)		 "I play tennis" ➡️ he played tennis.
Present Continuous
(is/am/are writing)		 ➡️ Past Continuous
(was/were writing)		 "I am playing" ➡️ he was playing.
Present Perfect
(has/have written)		 ➡️ Past Perfect
(had written)		 "I have played" ➡️ he had played.
Simple Past
(wrote)		 ➡️ Past Perfect
(had written)		 "I played" ➡️ he had played.
Will / Can / May ➡️ Would / Could / Might "I will win" ➡️ he would win.

TRAP ALERT! (Cambridge Favorite)

If the reporting verb is in the Present Tense (e.g., says, tells) or Future Tense (e.g., will say), the tense inside the quotes DOES NOT CHANGE!

  • She says, "I like apples." ➡️ She says that she likes apples. (Still present!)

Time & Place Ninjas

Because time has passed since the person spoke, words about 'now' and 'here' must change!

  • Now ➡️ Then
  • Today ➡️ That day
  • Tomorrow ➡️ The next day
  • Yesterday ➡️ The previous day / The day before
  • Here ➡️ There
  • This / These ➡️ That / Those

Reporting Questions

Questions turn into normal statements. No more question marks (?). Use asked or inquired.

Yes/No Questions (Use if/whether) "Do you like tea?" ➡️ He asked if I liked tea.
W-Questions (Keep the W-word) "Where do you live?" ➡️ He asked where I lived.

Commands & Requests

For orders, use verbs like ordered, told, requested. Connect with "to" or "not to".

Positive Command "Open the door!" ➡️ He ordered me to open the door.
Negative Command "Don't run!" ➡️ He told me not to run.
